Wild Color Peroxide – Oxidizing Emulsion Cream

Oxidizing emulsion cream

  • Wild Color™ Oxygen 2,5 vol (0,75%) is the ideal partner for Acid colors. A delicate developer for a light effect: it revives tone, adds shine and enhances reflections, always respecting the hair.
  • Wild Color™ Oxygen 5 vol (1,5%) is the right choice for toning. A gentle developer that deposits color with minimal lift, neutralizes unwanted tones and leaves the hair soft, shiny and evenly toned.
  • Wild Color™ Oxygen 10 vol (3%) is ideal for darker shades and tone-on-tone coloring on the same level. It gives full, rich color without lightening, for a deep, long-lasting and brilliant result.
  • Wild Color™ Oxygen 20 vol (6%) is the most versatile: it ensures full grey coverage, lifts the hair by one level and, used with bleach, provides a mild lightening of 2–3 levels with even, reliable results.
  • Wild Color™ Oxygen 30 vol (9%) lifts the hair by 1–2 levels, by 2–3 levels when mixed with super-lighteners and, used with bleach, gives a medium-intensity lightening of 4–5 levels for bright, even results.
  • Wild Color™ Oxygen 40 vol (12%) lifts the hair by 2 levels, by 3–4 levels when mixed with super-lighteners and, used with bleach, gives a strong lightening of 6–7 levels for maximum lift and vibrant results.

How to use Wild Color Oxygen

BLENDING

White hair less than 30%: 1:2 with Oxygen

White hair over 30%: 1:1,5 (1/4 natural base + 3/4 Reflection color)

White hair over 50%: 1:1,5 (1/3 Natural Base + 2/3 Reflection color)

White hair over 70%: 1:1,5 (1/2 Natural Base + 1/2 Reflection color)

Wild Color super lighteners: 1:3 with Oxygen

Wild Color toners, correctors, intensifiers: They are activated by Oxygen and the dosage depends on the application (1:2).

Wild Color comes with a creamy OXIDANT, rich in emollients and easy to apply.

It has a reduced sensitizing power.
