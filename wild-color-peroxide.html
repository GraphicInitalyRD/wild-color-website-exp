Wild Color Peroxide – Oxidizing Emulsion Cream

Oxidizing emulsion cream

Wild Color™ Oxygen is the ideal complement to Wild Color™ Coloring creams. A creamy, emollient-rich formulation that is easy to apply, has reduced sensitizing power and an excellent overall cosmetic result. Available in 2,5 – 5 – 10 – 20 – 30 – 40 volumes.

Wild Color™ Oxygen is the ideal complement to Wild Color™ Coloring creams. A creamy, emollient-rich formulation that is easy to apply, has reduced sensitizing power and an excellent overall cosmetic result.

How to use Wild Color Oxygen

COLOR USAGE

Wild Color Permanent, Ammonia free and All free colors are specially formulated to ensure 100% coverage of grey hair.

Application time from 25 minutes to 45 minutes in accordance with the desired level of lightening and coverage.

BLENDING

White hair less than 30%: 1:2 with Oxygen

White hair over 30%: 1:1,5 (1/4 natural base + 3/4 Reflection color)

White hair over 50%: 1:1,5 (1/3 Natural Base + 2/3 Reflection color)

White hair over 70%: 1:1,5 (1/2 Natural Base + 1/2 Reflection color)

Wild Color super lighteners: 1:3 with Oxygen

Wild Color toners, correctors, intensifiers: They are activated by Oxygen and the dosage depends on the application (1:2).

Wild Color comes with a creamy OXIDANT, rich in emollients and easy to apply.

It has a reduced sensitizing power.

OXYGEN USAGE

2,5 volumes (0.75%): for acid colors

5 volumes (1.5%): to tone.

10 volumes (3%): to darken / color on the same level.

20 volumes (6%): to cover grey hair; to lighten by one level; for mild bleaching of 2-3 levels.

25 volumes (7.5%): to cover grey hair with the use of Ammonia Free and All Free colors (mix in equal parts 20 vol and 30 vol oxygens)

30 volumes (9%): for lightening 1-2 levels; for lightening 2-3 levels with super-lighteners; for medium intensity bleaching of 4-5 levels.

40 volumes (12%): for lightening 2 levels; for lightening 3-4 levels with super-lighteners; for strong bleaching of 6-7 levels.
