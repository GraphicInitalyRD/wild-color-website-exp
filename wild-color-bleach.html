Wild Color Bleach – Bleaching Powder Plex Nine+

Bleaching Powder Plex Nine+

Wild Color™ Plex Nine+ Bleaching Powder is a high-quality bleaching powder combining maximum lightening power down to the darkest levels with a very high degree of hair preservation during application. Enriched with an innovative protective complex for optimal safeguarding of hair during bleaching, keeping it structurally intact, and Maltose to help achieve the magnificent cool highlights currently in vogue. Wild Color™ Plex Nine+ Bleaching Powder fits any application while neutralizing yellowish undertones. It provides high lightening power up to nine levels, while its unique protective complex nourishes and preserves the hair from the inside out, allowing you to achieve results you’ve only dreamed of.

USAGE: Mix (1:2) in a plastic bowl with Wild Color™ Oxidizing Emulsion Cream at 10, 20, 30 or 40 volumes, depending on the desired lightening. The versatility of the Powder also allows for 1:1 dilution up to 1:2.5 as needed. Apply, with a brush or freehand, the resulting cream on the parts to be lightened. Indicative application times (in relation to the peroxide used): medium bleaching 10 to 15 minutes; strong bleaching and mèches 15 to 30 minutes. Recommended for use at 20 vol for a maximum of 45 minutes of application.
