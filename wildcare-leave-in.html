Wildcare Leave-in – Wild Care™ Extraordinary Mask 10 in 1 – Coming soon

Wild Care™ Extraordinary Mask 10 in 1 is a leave-in product, for all hair types: 1. repairs damaged hair 2. disciplines frizz 3. protects against heat 4. facilitates straightening 5. provides extreme shine 6. protects color from fading 7. detangles hair 8. adds body and volume 9. prevents split ends 10. maintains style longer. A multifunctional hair spray, an innovative treatment combining the consistency and properties of a mask with the convenience and speed of a spray, for all the advantages of a quick no-rinse hair mask. Available in 250 ml size.

Usage: Apply to damp hair, massaging the most damaged parts and move on to styling.
