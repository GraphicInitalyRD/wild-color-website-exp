Wild Color Shampoo – Anti-Yellow Shampoo Extra Violet Pigment

Anti-Yellow Shampoo – Extra Violet Pigment

Especially for acid colors.

Wild Color™ Extra Blonde Shampoo counteracts the typical yellow pigment and any golden reflections. It revives bleached or highlighted hair and white, gray or very light blonde hair. Available in 2 sizes: 995 ml and 300 ml.

USAGE: Wear appropriate gloves and apply to wet hair, emulsify and massage gently. Leave on for 1-5 minutes, emulsify and rinse. Repeat if necessary.
