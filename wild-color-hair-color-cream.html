Wild Color™ Direct Color is available in 18 great colors: Violet, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Anthracite, Steel, Ice, Bordeaux, Fuchsia, Aqua, Rose, Mint, Lavender, Turquoise, Peach and Clear.

For bright, vivid colors, lighten hair with Wild Color™ Plex Nine+ Bleaching Powder or Wild Color™ Blue Lightening Cream up to the recommended level: Blue, Violet, Steel, Ice, Fuchsia, Rose, Mint, Lavender, Turquoise, Peach and Clear: pre-lighten to a level 9 or 10. Recommended application time: up to 30 minutes. Green, Orange, Red, Yellow, Anthracite, Bordeaux and Aqua: pre-lighten to a level 7 or 8. Recommended application time: up to 30 minutes. The Clear color can be used as a “glaze effect” polisher, or to create different shades of pastel color, using it in combination with any other color available. Wild Color™ Direct Color must be applied to dry hair, not to the scalp and without mixing with water or oxidizing emulsions.
APPLICATION: Shampoo and then dry the hair. Section the hair and saturate each area thoroughly before moving on to the next. Once the recommended application time is over, rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water. Do not use any type of shampoo. Next, move on to styling using any products you like. We recommend avoiding straighteners and similar tools immediately after applying Wild Color™ Direct Color to preserve color hold.
