Wild Color™ Hair Color Cream

Wild Color™ Permanent Hair Color is the benchmark for professional color: dependable, consistent performance, application after application. Its formula uses ammonia progressively, raising the concentration as shades get lighter, so every shade holds just what it needs to reach the result, with maximum respect for the hair.

Expect full, even color from roots to ends and 100% gray coverage, with a finish that is rich, glossy and true to the chosen shade.

In the salon, an extensive palette opens up endless combinations for every taste, idea and need. Colorists can tailor each service with precision and count on results that faithfully match previous ones.

Permanent Hair Color brings reliability, versatility and color quality together: the essential salon tool, ready to meet every client’s needs.

Wild Color™ Ammonia Free is permanent color for those who want a complete, professional result from an ammonia-free formula. The name says it all: traditional hair color, reimagined, with no compromise on performance.

Alternative alkalizing agents take the place of ammonia and do the same technical job, preparing the hair fiber so the pigments can work in depth. The outcome matches traditional color, with complete gray coverage.

Every pigment in the range has been carefully selected for a wide choice of shades and endless combinations. Colorists can create custom shades, from the most natural to the most sophisticated, and count on consistent, effective results.

Wild Color™ Ammonia Free is the ideal answer for salons that want a complete permanent color service: performance, comfort and versatility, together in one product.

Wild Color™ All Free Hair Color is professional color with the utmost respect for the hair, even the most sensitive. The formula is free from ammonia, parabens, PPD, resorcinol and silicones: a deliberate choice, built for high performance.

Specific, high-quality alternative pigments make it possible to leave these ingredients out without losing color quality. Each one is selected through dedicated testing and precise ratios between color components, for top intensity, evenness and shine.

Coloring becomes a more pleasant, comfortable experience, for clients and for professionals in the salon every day. Colorists keep full control of the result and work with confidence, even for clients who look closely at what goes into their products.

Wild Color™ All Free brings color performance and respect for the hair fiber together: the ideal choice for salons serving increasingly conscious clients.

Wild Color™ Acid Color is a demi-permanent system designed for precise color results that fully respect the integrity of the hair. Its low-alkalinity formula works exclusively with a 2.5-volume oxidizing emulsion, for a controlled oxidative process with no significant cuticle opening and no lightening.

The natural base stays untouched. Color deposits selectively, mainly on the more porous, pre-lightened areas, so toning happens exactly where it is needed. The result is even and predictable, with no banding or spotting: perfect for bleached blondes, balayage and advanced lightening.

In the salon it is versatile and reliable, even straight at the backwash, and the mixing ratio lets colorists tune the intensity to every client.

The essential tool for modern toning: color performance, respect for the hair fiber and natural-looking results, in perfect balance.

Wild Color™ Direct Color is available in 18 great colors: Violet, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Anthracite, Steel, Ice, Bordeaux, Fuchsia, Aqua, Rose, Mint, Lavender, Turquoise, Peach and Clear.

For bright, vivid colors, lighten hair with Wild Color™ Plex Nine+ Bleaching Powder or Wild Color™ Blue Lightening Cream up to the recommended level: Blue, Violet, Steel, Ice, Fuchsia, Rose, Mint, Lavender, Turquoise, Peach and Clear: pre-lighten to a level 9 or 10. Recommended application time: up to 30 minutes. Green, Orange, Red, Yellow, Anthracite, Bordeaux and Aqua: pre-lighten to a level 7 or 8. Recommended application time: up to 30 minutes. The Clear color can be used as a “glaze effect” polisher, or to create different shades of pastel color, using it in combination with any other color available. Wild Color™ Direct Color must be applied to dry hair, not to the scalp and without mixing with water or oxidizing emulsions.
APPLICATION: Shampoo and then dry the hair. Section the hair and saturate each area thoroughly before moving on to the next. Once the recommended application time is over, rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water. Do not use any type of shampoo. Next, move on to styling using any products you like. We recommend avoiding straighteners and similar tools immediately after applying Wild Color™ Direct Color to preserve color hold.
