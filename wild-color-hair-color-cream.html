Wild Color Hair Color Cream

Color permanent, ammonia free, all free & acid color

  • Wild Color™ is a coloring cream enriched with jojoba oil, olive oil and argan oil.
  • It is available in a 180 ml size (enough for a minimum of 4 applications)
  • and offers a wide range of colors:
  • Among the ammonia colors we include:
  • 10 series of basic natural colors, which is unique in the world,
  • 69 fashion colors and 10 super lightening colors.
  • 33 ammonia-free colors.
  • 4 ammonia-free soft toners.
  • 15 all-free colors.
  • 18 direct colors.
  • 6 colors specifically studied for men.
  • 10 colors including intensifiers, correctors and toners.

Direct color

  • Wild Color™ Direct Color is available in 18 great colors:
  • Violet, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue,
  • Anthracite, Steel, Ice, Bordeaux, Fuchsia, Aqua,
  • Rose, Mint, Lavender, Turquoise, Peach and Clear.
  • For bright, vivid colors, lighten hair with Wild Color™ Plex Nine+ Bleaching Powder
  • or Wild Color™ Blue Lightening Cream up to the recommended level:
  • Blue, Violet, Steel, Ice, Fuchsia, Rose, Mint, Lavender, Turquoise, Peach and Clear:
  • pre-lighten to a level 9 or 10.
  • Recommended application time: up to 30 minutes.
  • Green, Orange, Red, Yellow, Anthracite, Bordeaux and Aqua:
  • pre-lighten to a level 7 or 8.
  • Recommended application time: up to 30 minutes.
  • The Clear color can be used as a “glaze effect” polisher,
  • or to create different shades of pastel color,
  • using it in combination with any other color available.
  • Wild Color™ Direct Color must be applied to dry hair, not to the scalp
  • and without mixing with water or oxidizing emulsions.
  • APPLICATION: Shampoo and then dry the hair.
  • Section the hair and saturate each area thoroughly before moving on to the next.
  • Once the recommended application time is over, rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.
  • Do not use any type of shampoo.
  • Next, move on to styling using any products you like.
  • We recommend avoiding straighteners and similar tools
  • immediately after applying Wild Color™ Direct Color to preserve color hold.
