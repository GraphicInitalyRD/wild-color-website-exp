Wildcare Restructuring Products – Wild Care™ Restructuring Mask & Shampoo – Coming soon

Wild Care™ Restructuring Shampoo gently cleanses and prepares scalp and hair for the next steps of the restructuring treatment. Its keratin-based formula provides an intense and deep restructuring and regenerating action for the hair fiber. It contains natural extracts providing additional shine and volume, leaving hair feeling soft and scented. Wild Care™ Restructuring Shampoo is available in two sizes: one liter and 250 ml.

Usage: Apply to damp hair, massaging into the most damaged areas, leave on for a few minutes, rinse and repeat if necessary.

Wild Care™ Restructuring Mask with its acidifying action, strengthens the structure of the hair by compacting the cuticles. Its keratin-infused formula helps restructure and regenerate the hair fiber. Nourished and strengthened hair regains strength and softness from roots to ends. Wild Care™ Restructuring Mask is available in a 250ml size.

Usage: Apply to damp hair from root to tips using a comb, leave on for 5-10 minutes, then rinse and style.
