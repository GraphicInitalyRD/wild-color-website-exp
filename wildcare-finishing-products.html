Wildcare Finishing Products – Wild Style™ – Coming soon

Wild Style™ Soft Fix Hair Spray

Locks your chosen hairstyle in place with a light, airy film. Dries quickly and leaves no residue. Provides lightness and shine. Soft and silky hair. Available in 300 ml size.

Usage: Spray holding the bottle 25-30 cm from the hair. For volume, spray on roots before styling.

Wild Style™ Performance Mousse

A creamy foam with medium hold. Fixes styling and is suitable for all hair types. Ideal for both a silky smooth look and for defining curls and waves. Available in 300 ml size.

Usage: Shake well before use. Dab off excess water and apply evenly, then blow dry as desired.

Wild Style™ Strong Fix Hair Spray

A professional hairspray with an effective, long-lasting hold. Instantly sets and defines, even those extreme looks. Easy to remove. Leaves hair shiny and pleasant to touch. Available in 300 ml size.

Usage: Spray at a distance of 25-30 cm on dry or damp hair. For extra volume, spray on damp hair at the roots before styling. For extreme hold, spray on dry hair before using a straightener or flat iron.
